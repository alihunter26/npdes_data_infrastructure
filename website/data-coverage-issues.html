Data Coverage Issues

Known data issues, anomalies, and caveats across the NPDES / ICIS data sets used by this site — what's wrong or easy to misread in the raw files, and what it means for the facility-month panel specifically. See Data Documentation for what each file contains and Temporal Coverage for which years each variable actually has data.

NPDES_FORMAL_ENFORCEMENT_ACTIONS

High

Variable(s): FED_PENALTY_ASSESSED_AMT SETTLEMENT_ENTERED_DATE

One enforcement action (single ACTIVITY_ID) is stored as one row per permit it touches: same fine, same date repeated. PRASA = 135 rows of $1,024,427 (measured); Deepwater Horizon $3.35B recorded as lead + non-lead records with different state/compliance allocations (verified vs ECHO).

Panel impact: Summing penalties/actions naively double-counts multi-permit cases; aggregate/dedupe at ACTIVITY_ID.

ICIS_FACILITIES

High (universe) / Low (panel)

Variable(s): FACILITY_UIN ↔ NPDES_ID

10.5% of FRS IDs map to >1 NPDES ID (measured), inflated by generic umbrellas (one ND UIN = 361 unrelated permits).

Panel impact: Dangerous universe-wide, but near 1:1 for major-individual — only 84 facilities hold >1 permit (one has 4, four have 3, rest 2).

NPDES_CATCHMENTS

Medium

Variable(s): catchment record / NHDPLUSID

Covers inland surface waters only: coastal/offshore facilities have no record and silently drop from inner-join spatial merges (no error).

Panel impact: ~7% (549) of major-individual permits (coastal/marine) vanish from catchment joins → anti-join to catch them.

ICIS_PERMITS

Medium

Variable(s): PERMIT_STATUS_CODE EXPIRATION_DATE TERMINATION_DATE

No facility open/close field; existence inferred from permit lifecycle. ADC (admin-continued, ~243k) = "expired" but still active, so EXPIRATION_DATE overstates closures. Permit ≠ facility.

Panel impact: Entry/exit derived from permit dates; must treat ADC/EXP-continued as active or you mis-date exits.

NPDES_NAICS, NPDES_SICS

Medium

Variable(s): NAICS_CODE SIC_CODE

NAICS ~65% missing; SIC ~99% for majors but ~39% of all permits have neither (measured). Missingness flat across vintages (not date-gated).

Panel impact: NAICS unusable panel-wide → use SIC (~99% in panel). Newest entrants worst-coded.

NPDES_NAICS + NPDES_SICS

Medium

Variable(s): NAICS_CODE ↔ SIC_CODE

No clean crosswalk: many-to-many (NAICS 221320 → 88 SICs; SIC 1542 → 115 NAICS) (measured).

Panel impact: Can't convert/reconcile the two systems; pick one and use its primary code.

bulk enforcement files (vs case-report API)

Low

Variable(s): SEP value, compliance-action cost, cost recovery

Absent from bulk files: only in the case-report API (PRASA: $1M penalty vs $195M compliance cost).

Panel impact: Bulk penalty totals understate true case weight.

NPDES_FORMAL/INFORMAL_ENFORCEMENT_ACTIONS

Medium

Variable(s): FED_PENALTY_ASSESSED_AMT STATE_LOCAL_PENALTY_AMT

Mostly missing/zero: the majority of actions are informal (no penalty).

Panel impact: Don't use penalty $ as a general enforcement outcome; use action counts / SNC flags.

NPDES_DMRS

Medium (if using DMR)

Variable(s): DMR_VALUE_NMBR (non-receipt)

Coverage majors ~84% vs minors ~7% (FY2025) (measured); the 2016 eRule pulls minors in → non-stationary.

Panel impact: Majors well-covered; if adding DMR/violations, control for/flag the ~2016 reporting break.

NPDES_EFF_VIOLATIONS

Medium

Variable(s): whole file

Derived & lossy — only violating rows, no compliant reports. Absent facility-year = "no violation," NOT "no discharge."

Panel impact: Can't compute rates/averages; violation counts only.

NPDES_EFF_VIOLATIONS

Medium

Variable(s): EXCEEDENCE_PCT RNC_* NODI_CODE DAYS_LATE

Populated only for their specific violation subtype (conditionally defined, not truly missing).

Panel impact: Condition on VIOLATION_TYPE_CODE before aggregating or means/sums mislead.

all CSVs (esp. ICIS_FACILITIES, ICIS_PERMITS)

Medium (technical)

Variable(s): quoted text fields (FACILITY_NAME, RAD_WBD_HUC12S…)

Legal-but-tricky CSV — embedded commas ("BP EXPLORATION&PRODUCTION, INC") and embedded newlines inside quoted fields (a multi-line RAD_WBD_HUC12S value spilled "Waterloo Municipal Airport" fragments) — break naive cut -d,/awk -F, and line-by-line splitting, silently shifting columns or spawning fake "rows." The data is fine; proper parsers reassemble it.

Panel impact: Use a real CSV parser (read.csv/fread/Python csv); never delimiter- or line-split.

ICIS_PERMITS

Low

Variable(s): EXTERNAL_PERMIT_NMBR VERSION_NMBR

Individual permits keep the same NPDES_ID across reissuance (versioned, ~52% >1 version); general coverages get renumbered each cycle (NDR100000→NDR110000).

Panel impact: Individual IDs are stable → coherent time series (general renumber not in panel).

ICIS_PERMITS

Low

Variable(s): MAJOR_MINOR_STATUS_FLAG

~3.6% of rows blank (measured).

Panel impact: Those facilities can't be classified; drop from major-restricted sample.

NPDES_NAICS, NPDES_SICS

Low

Variable(s): NAICS_CODE SIC_CODE

Snapshots (not time-varying); also ~49k facilities have >1 SIC, ~9k >1 NAICS (measured) (multi-activity sites).

Panel impact: Treat industry as time-invariant; collapse with the primary code.

ICIS_FACILITIES

Low (panel) / High (general)

Variable(s): GEOCODE_LATITUDE/LONGITUDE

General/minor sites often un-geocoded → city/county-centroid or placeholder coords; many "facilities" at one point. Conflicting geocodes for offshore/corporate permits (BP: office vs offshore ~370 km apart; inland-TX "99999" placeholder).

Panel impact: Only ~0.5% of major-individual have invalid geocodes (measured) — good within panel, bad outside. Check LATLONG_TYPE.

NPDES_PERM_FEATURE_COORDS

Low (panel)

Variable(s): PERM_FEATURE_NMBR / coords

A permit can be a spatial cloud — GMG290110 = 76 outfalls over ~510 km (measured). Individual permits: 44% multi-outfall but clustered at one site.

Panel impact: Individual outfalls cluster → collapsing to a point is fine; extreme spread is general/offshore.

multiple files

Low

Variable(s): MAJOR_MINOR_FLAG vs MAJOR_MINOR_STATUS_FLAG

Column names differ across files (outfalls layer vs permits); ECHO renames columns.

Panel impact: Join defensively on candidate names.

NPDES_EFF_VIOLATIONS

Low

Variable(s): ADJUSTED_DMR_VALUE_NMBR

Essentially always empty — 6 of 46,361,587 rows (measured); effluent trading is negligible.

Panel impact: Ignore the field.

NPDES_ATTAINS_AU_SUMMARIES

Low

Variable(s): REPORTINGCYCLE / impairment

Snapshot, not a time series — only the latest cycle per unit (0 of 813k unit-pairs have >1) (measured); no impairment history.

Panel impact: Water quality = time-invariant covariate; can't study change over the window.

NPDES_CATCHMENTS

Low

Variable(s): (no time column)

Static facility→catchment spatial crosswalk (physical hydrography).

Panel impact: Time-invariant; fine to join once.
