NPDES High-Level Overview

A plain-language map of the datasets behind this site — what each source covers, what it’s useful for, and what one row represents. Start here before diving into the file-by-file detail in Data Documentation or the per-variable stats in Data Summaries.

SourceWhat’s there?Row
ICIS-NPDES Data on every US facility that holds a permit to discharge water. Contains reference information on each facility (location, permit history, and industry). Also contains information on permit violations from missed deadlines, failure to follow court orders, and one-off pollutants, and the resulting enforcement. This is a reference file for the other datasets, and can be joined through the NPDES_ID (identifies permit). One facility, permit, inspection, violation, enforcement
ICIS-NPDES 2: Effluent Violations Records every instance where a facility discharges more pollution than legally allowed by the permit. Can be used to show historical trends on which facilities have had the most exceedance events. Includes millions of observations of individual violations. One violation
Master General Permits Lists general permits issued by the EPA (permits applied broadly to similar facilities operating in the same region). Includes 2,823 observations, with the vast majority coming from state agencies but some from the EPA. Includes information on the type of permit. This can be linked to individual facilities in the ICIS data using MASTER_GENERAL_PERMITS as a join key. One general permit
Discharge Points Identifies locations where facilities release their treated or partially treated water. Data is listed by discharge point (not facility), and facilities can have multiple discharge points. It includes latitude and longitude coordinates for each point to specify exactly where pollutants are potentially being emitted. One discharge point (outfall)
Permit Limit Limits written into each facility’s permit for every monitored substance. For each pollutant and each discharge point, it records the maximum allowable concentration or quantity, the unit of measurement, and how often the facility is required to measure and report. One limit (at a specific discharge point, under a specific permit)
DMR (Discharge Monitoring Report) Self-reported discharge measurements submitted by facilities (what they released into waterways during each reporting period). Files can be downloaded by fiscal year. One measurement of a pollutant (at one discharge point, during one reporting period)
Monitoring Data Download Shows the regulatory limit of emissions and the actual discharge by facility, allowing comparison between what the facility is allowed to do and what they actually do. Can be downloaded for a specific facility for a specific period of time (dating back to 2017). Data for each facility contains each of the individual sources and the individual pollutants. This is permit data and DMR data joined for a specific facility. One measurement of a pollutant (at one discharge point, during one reporting period)
Catchment Index and Assorted Waters These three datasets link each facility to the specific body of water it drains into and connect it to water quality assessments. It contains latitude and longitude coordinates of facilities to see which waterways sit downstream of each facility, and rates the health of the waterways. Shows the actual effect of potential pollutants on local waterways. Discharge point, catchment and water body, facility and downstream water body

For row counts, key variables, and join logic file-by-file, see Data Documentation. For which years each dataset actually has data, see Temporal Coverage.
