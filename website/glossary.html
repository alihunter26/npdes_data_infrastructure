Glossary & Key Terms

Acronyms and terms used throughout this site's data summaries, briefs, and documentation, listed alphabetically.

AFO

Animal Feeding Operation

Any facility that confines animals for 45+ days per year where vegetation is not sustained in the confinement area.

CAFO

Concentrated Animal Feeding Operation

A subset of AFOs that meet size thresholds or discharge to waterways, making them subject to NPDES permitting requirements.

CWA

Clean Water Act

The 1972 federal law that regulates water pollution in the US by controlling what pollutants can be discharged into waterways.

DMR

Discharge Monitoring Report

The form facilities submit (usually monthly) reporting their actual discharge measurements against their permit limits.

ECHO

Enforcement and Compliance History Online

EPA's public database where you can find permit, inspection, enforcement, and DMR data for all NPDES-permitted facilities.

HUC

Hydrologic Unit Code

The numbering system used in the WBD to identify watersheds at different scales, with HUC-8 and HUC-12 being most common in research.

ICIS

Integrated Compliance Information System

The backend EPA database that stores all NPDES compliance and enforcement data, which ECHO pulls from for public display.

NHD

National Hydrography Dataset

A USGS geospatial dataset mapping all US rivers, streams, and water bodies including flow direction, used to trace upstream/downstream relationships.

NPDES

National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System

The CWA's permitting program that requires any facility discharging pollutants into US waters to obtain a permit with specific limits.

POTW

Publicly Owned Treatment Works

A municipally-owned wastewater treatment plant that treats sewage and discharges treated effluent under an NPDES permit.

SIC

Standard Industrial Classification

A federal code system that categorizes facilities by industry type, used in ECHO to filter and identify facility types in NPDES data.

SNC

Significant Noncompliance

EPA's designation for facilities with serious or repeated permit violations that triggers priority enforcement response.

TMDL

Total Maximum Daily Load

The maximum amount of a pollutant a water body can receive and still meet water quality standards, set under CWA Section 303(d).

WBD

Watershed Boundary Dataset

A USGS geospatial dataset defining watershed boundaries at multiple scales (HUC levels), used to assign facilities and monitoring stations to watersheds.

WQP

Water Quality Portal

A national database aggregating ambient water quality measurements from USGS, EPA STORET, and other monitoring sources.
